I am in the fourth year of the B.B.A. LL.B. (Hons.) programme at Amity University, Uttar Pradesh. My work so far has sat deliberately on both sides of the profession — the advisory desk and the courtroom — because I do not think either is fully legible without the other.

At Dua Associates I researched the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, directors’ duties and the Draft Telecommunications Rules, 2026. At Fox Mandal I drafted non-disclosure agreements, an appointment letter and a FEMA compliance checklist, and wrote an article on the responsibilities of the Board for the firm’s knowledge management initiative. In the chambers of a Special Public Prosecutor for the CBI, I worked on Prevention of Corruption Act matters and then watched them argued at the Delhi High Court.

What I take from that range is a habit rather than a specialism: read the provision first, find out what the courts have actually done with it, and write it down so that someone else can use it.

Dua Associates

Corporate Advisory & Dispute Resolution

A Tier 1 corporate firm, and the most recent and most demanding of my placements. The brief ran across insolvency, company law, arbitration and the regulation of technology and telecommunications — frequently within the same week.

  • Corporate, insolvency and commercial research. The Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, including the legislative intent and retrospective applicability of Section 96(4); winding-up under the Companies Act, 1956 and 2013; directors’ duties and liabilities; termination of Managing Directors; complaints under Section 166; and the liability of independent directors.
  • Commercial disputes and arbitration. The unilateral formation of sub-committees, reliance on internal reports to deny contractual payments, the judicial interpretation of finance costs, and review of arbitration correspondence.
  • Technology and telecom regulation. Cyber-crime offences under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023; unlawful interception and call tapping; and the Draft Telecommunications (Spectrum Assignment by Administrative Process) Rules, 2026.
  • Case preparation. Supreme Court and NCLAT judgments, the retrospective applicability of clarificatory amendments, correctness of contents in affidavits under the Commercial Courts Act, and electricity regulatory disputes on PPAs and Wheeling & Banking Agreements.
  • Researched insurance law, including fire insurance claims where the insured godown location was changed without intimation to the insurer, and the role, authority and liability of insurance agents and sub-agents.
  • Examined civil procedure and banking questions under Order IX Rule 9 read with Section 151 of the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908, and whether one holder of an “either or survivor” joint fixed deposit may unilaterally discharge or alter it.
  • Researched the freezing of bank accounts in cybercrime investigations — the scope of police powers under Section 106(3) BNSS and Section 102 CrPC, the legality of blanket freezing orders, unilateral debits from frozen accounts, and violations of Articles 14, 19(1)(g), 21 and 300A.
  • Analysed the MSMED Act, 2006 from the buyer’s perspective under Sections 15 and 16, covering adjustment and reconciliation clauses, set-off of liabilities, and whether liability crystallises on admitted invoices or only after adjudication.
  • Drafted written arguments in a labour law dispute, prepared case briefs, researched anticipatory bail, and ran searches on opposite parties’ assets and pending litigation.
  • Researched DDCA membership rules and fraud judgments, lateral appointments and deputations to senior posts including PIL maintainability, and condonation of delay before the NCDRC under the 30 + 15 day written statement framework.
  • Researched FDI policy and sectoral caps under the Foreign Exchange Management (Non-Debt Instruments) Rules, 2019, and FPI eligibility to subscribe to non-convertible debentures under the SEBI (FPI) Regulations, 2019.
  • Examined the fiduciary duties of the Board under the Companies Act, 2013 and the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, and IP licensing restrictions on foreign licensors under FEMA, 1999.
  • Prepared a FEMA compliance checklist for FDI transactions covering Form FC-GPR, FC-TRS, ECB and FLA reporting, and examined the penalty framework under the Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008 for non-filing of Forms 8 and 11.
  • Drafted two non-disclosure agreements, a standard appointment letter, and a due diligence list of documents spanning shareholders’ agreements, share purchase agreements, memoranda of association, founders’ agreements and powers of attorney.
  • Reviewed an employment appointment letter, identifying gaps across probation, termination, confidentiality and post-employment clauses, and a Maharashtra Pollution Control Board consent to operate under the Water and Air Acts.
  • Authored a firm-assigned article on the roles and responsibilities of the Board of Directors under the Companies Act, 2013, in Bluebook citation format, for the firm’s knowledge management initiative.
